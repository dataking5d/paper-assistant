# English checks

- Preserve formal academic register; do not force conversational tone.
- Keep passive voice when the actor is unknown or unimportant; do not invent an actor.
- Keep hedges (`may`, `suggest`, `associated`, `cannot rule out`) and their strength.
- Do not ban every dash or every triad; keep them when they carry structure or emphasis.
- Do not add citations, data, examples, or institutional names.
- Treat technical terms (`robust estimator`, `closed-loop control`) as field vocabulary, not AI tells.
- One-line closers are candidates, not errors; keep them when they add a fact or emphasis.
