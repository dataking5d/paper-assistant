# Academic Phrasebank — 学术短语库

## 引言部分

### 建立研究领域重要性
- "X has been a central concern in Y research..."
- "The study of X has gained considerable attention due to..."
- "Understanding X is essential for Y because..."

### 指出研究缺口
- "However, little is known about..."
- "Previous studies have not adequately addressed..."
- "A key question remains unanswered: ..."

### 陈述研究问题/目标
- "This study aims to investigate..."
- "The present paper seeks to answer the following question: ..."
- "We address this gap by examining..."

## 文献综述部分

### 整合已有发现
- "A growing body of evidence suggests that..."
- "Collectively, these findings indicate..."
- "The literature on X can be organized around three themes: ..."

### 指出局限
- "However, these studies are limited by..."
- "An important limitation of prior work is..."
- "These findings should be interpreted with caution because..."

## 讨论部分

### 与已有研究对话
- "Consistent with X's findings, we observed that..."
- "In contrast to X, our results suggest..."
- "Our findings extend X's work by showing..."

### 陈述含义
- "These results have important implications for..."
- "From a practical standpoint, these findings suggest..."
- "Theoretically, this contributes to our understanding of..."

## 结论部分

### 总结贡献
- "This study makes three main contributions to the literature..."
- "Taken together, our findings demonstrate..."
- "In summary, the present research provides evidence that..."

## 中文对应

### 引言
- "X近年来受到广泛关注..."
- "已有研究主要聚焦于...而对...关注不足"
- "本文旨在..."

### 文献综述
- "已有研究从以下三个方面展开..."
- "尽管已有丰富的研究成果，但仍存在以下不足..."

### 讨论
- "与X的发现一致..."
- "与X的结论不同，本研究发现..."
- "这些发现对...具有以下启示..."

### 结论
- "本文的主要贡献在于..."
- "综上，本研究表明..."
