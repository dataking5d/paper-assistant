# Chinese-to-English Polish

## Common Chinglish patterns to fix

### Pattern 1: Missing subject
中文: "通过分析发现..." → 英文: "The analysis revealed..." (not "Through analysis found...")

### Pattern 2: Run-on sentences
中文用逗号连接多个从句 → 英文用句号分句

### Pattern 3: Nominalization overuse
"make an improvement to" → "improve"
"conduct an investigation of" → "investigate"

### Pattern 4: Article errors
- Check every singular countable noun for a/an/the
- Chinese authors often omit articles where English requires them

### Pattern 5: Tense consistency
- Literature review → present perfect or present
- Methods/Results → past tense
- Discussion → present for interpretation, past for referring to results
