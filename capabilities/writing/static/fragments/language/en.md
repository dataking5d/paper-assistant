# English Academic Writing

## Language conventions
- Use formal academic register
- Prefer active voice over passive where appropriate
- Use first-person "we" for author actions (avoid "the author")
- Avoid contractions (don't, can't, won't → do not, cannot, will not)

## Sentence structure
- Average 15-25 words per sentence
- Vary sentence length for rhythm
- Topic sentence first in each paragraph
- Explicit logical connectors between sentences

## Verb calibration to evidence strength
- `demonstrate / show / establish` → requires strong direct evidence
- `suggest / indicate / imply` → trend-level or indirect evidence
- `may / might / could` → plausible but unverified mechanisms

## Paragraph structure
- One paragraph = one idea
- Opening: topic/claim sentence
- Body: evidence, examples, reasoning
- Closing: transition or summary (optional)

## Common pitfalls for Chinese authors
- Overly long sentences with nested clauses → split into 2-3 sentences
- Missing articles (a/an/the) → double-check every noun phrase
- Redundant pairs ("various different", "completely eliminate") → choose one
- Direct translation of Chinese academic phrases → use English academic conventions
